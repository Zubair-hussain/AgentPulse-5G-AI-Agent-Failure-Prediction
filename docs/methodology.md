# Methodology and limitations
Seed 2026, independent PRNG stream per shard. Shards modulo 10 assign 80% train, 10% validation, 10% test. Environment IDs are split-disjoint; validation and test deliberately have a modest rate-limit distribution shift. Latent complexity, load and instability drive 16 observable numerical signals. A stochastic logistic outcome with a CPU/rate-limit interaction creates imperfect labels. No outcome field is included in predictors. StandardScaler is fit on training only; F1 decision thresholds are tuned on validation only. Test results are for this simulator and do not measure real-world failure forecasting. The simulator does not model all temporal dependencies, task quality, causal inference or hallucinations. No independent human annotations or external traces. The prior baseline uses the same threshold selection as other models. The shipped model is logistic regression for portable, auditable inference. Gradient boosting is evaluated but not serialized. Full-scale SGD supports training across every train shard in bounded memory; evaluation is sampled unless cap is zero. Confidence intervals and repeated-seed experiments are future work.
